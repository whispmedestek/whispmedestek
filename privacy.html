WhispMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

In short: your videos, audio, scripts, photos and location stay on your phone. WhispMe has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no servers that receive your content. We do not see, collect, store, sell or share it.

1. Who we are

WhispMe is an app published by its independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: whispme.destek@gmail.com.

2. What the app keeps – only on your device

  • Videos you record are saved to your Photos/gallery. The app also keeps a private copy in its own storage so you can play them in "My recordings".
  • Scripts you write or paste, and your settings (language, theme, font, speed, and similar).
  • Recording details shown in "My recordings": title, date, time, duration, format and, if you allowed location, where the recording started.

None of this is sent to us or to any server we operate. Removing an item in the app removes the app's own copy and entry. A video in your Photos stays there unless you choose "Also delete from Photos" or delete it yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes the app's own data.

3. Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Camera and microphone – to show your preview and record video with sound, including the optional microphone level meter. Audio and video are processed on your device only.
  • Photos / media library – to save recordings, to delete them when you ask, and (on iPhone, through the system picker) to let you choose a video to trim.
  • Location (optional, only while using the app) – when a recording starts, the app can note your approximate position and keep it on your device so you can see where the video was recorded. If you tap the 📍 button, your phone opens Google Maps with those coordinates; Google then receives them under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Motion sensors – to detect whether you hold the phone upright or sideways. Readings are used instantly on the device and are not stored.
  • Clipboard – read only when you tap "Paste".

You can change or withdraw any permission in your phone's Settings; the related feature will then stop working.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, profiling or tracking tools.
  • We do not upload, view, analyse, sell or share your recordings, scripts or location.

5. Third parties and things outside our control

  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle downloads, payments (if any) and their own statistics under their own policies.
  • Google Maps receives coordinates only if you tap 📍.
  • Device backups and Photos sync (for example iCloud or Google backup) may copy the app's data and your videos to Apple or Google if you turned those services on. This is your device setting, not something we control.
  • Sharing – if you upload or send a video to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn or anyone else, that is your action and that service's policy applies.

6. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to answer you and improve the app, and keep them only as long as needed (legitimate interest; where required, your consent). We do not share them except where the law requires.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your recordings, scripts or location, there is nothing in our systems to access or delete beyond any emails you have sent us. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698) you may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of such data, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority. Write to whispme.destek@gmail.com.

8. Children

WhispMe is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children; because the app collects no data, there is none to remove. Parents and guardians remain responsible for how minors record and share videos.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Use a screen lock and keep your phone updated. We recommend you keep your own backups.

10. Changes

If we add features that handle data – for example optional AI script writing, subscriptions or cloud backup – we will update this policy before release, explain exactly what is sent and why, and ask for your permission where required. The effective date above shows the latest version.

1. Biz kimiz

WhispMe, bağımsız geliştiricisi ("biz") tarafından yayınlanan bir uygulamadır. İletişim: whispme.destek@gmail.com.

2. Uygulamanın tuttukları – yalnızca cihazınızda

  • Yazdığınız veya yapıştırdığınız senaryolar ve ayarlarınız (dil, tema, yazı tipi, hız vb.).
  • "Kayıtlarım"da görünen kayıt bilgileri: başlık, tarih, saat, süre, format ve konum iznini verdiyseniz kaydın başladığı yer.

Bunların hiçbiri bize veya bizim işlettiğimiz bir sunucuya gönderilmez. Uygulamada bir öğeyi silmek, uygulamanın kendi kopyasını ve kaydını siler. Fotoğraflar'daki video, "Fotoğraflar'dan da sil"i seçmediğiniz veya kendiniz silmediğiniz sürece orada kalır. Uygulamayı kaldırmak uygulamanın kendi verilerini siler.

3. İzinler ve neden istendikleri

  • Fotoğraflar / medya kitaplığı – kayıtları kaydetmek, istediğinizde silmek ve (iPhone'da, sistem seçicisi üzerinden) kırpmak için bir video seçmenizi sağlamak için.
  • Konum (isteğe bağlı, yalnızca uygulama kullanılırken) – kayıt başlarken uygulama yaklaşık konumunuzu not edip yalnızca cihazınızda saklayabilir; böylece videonun nerede çekildiğini görürsünüz. 📍 düğmesine basarsanız telefonunuz bu koordinatlarla Google Haritalar'ı açar; koordinatları Google, kendi gizlilik politikası kapsamında alır.
  • Hareket sensörleri – telefonu dik mi yan mı tuttuğunuzu anlamak için. Okumalar anında cihazda kullanılır, saklanmaz.
  • Pano – yalnızca "Yapıştır"a bastığınızda okunur.

İzinleri telefonunuzun Ayarlar bölümünden değiştirebilir veya geri alabilirsiniz; ilgili özellik çalışmaz hâle gelir.

Yaşadığınız yere göre (örneğin AB GDPR'ı veya 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu kapsamında) bu verilere erişim, düzeltme veya silme talep edebilir, işlenmesine itiraz edebilir ve veri koruma otoritesine şikâyette bulunabilirsiniz. whispme.destek@gmail.com adresine yazabilirsiniz.

9. Güvenlik
