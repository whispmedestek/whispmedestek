2. Kısaca

Videoların, senaryoların ve ayarların yalnızca telefonunda saklanır. WhispMe'nin kendi sunucusu yoktur, hesap açman gerekmez ve bu içeriklerin hiçbiri bize gönderilmez. Siyah ekran prompter modu ücretsiz ve reklamsızdır. Kamerayla kayıt için ödüllü reklam izlenir (Google AdMob) ya da Premium abonelik alınır (Apple App Store / Google Play, RevenueCat aracılığıyla).

3. Yalnızca cihazında kalan veriler

  • Kamera ve mikrofon: önizleme ve video kaydı için. Kayıtlar galerine ve uygulamanın kendi klasörüne kaydedilir.
  • Fotoğraflar / galeri: kayıtlarını kaydetmek, kırpmak için video seçmek ve istediğinde silmek için.
  • Konum (isteğe bağlı): kaydın nerede başladığını cihazında not etmek için.
  • Hareket sensörü: telefonu dik mi yan mı tuttuğunu anlamak için; saklanmaz.
  • Senaryolar, ayarlar, ücretsiz süre bilgisi: uygulamanın cihazdaki deposunda.

5. İşleme amaçları ve hukuki sebepler

  • Abonelik ve Premium erişim: sözleşmenin kurulması ve ifası (KVKK m.5/2-c; GDPR m.6/1-b).

  • Reklam ölçümü, güvenlik ve kötüye kullanımın önlenmesi: meşru menfaat (KVKK m.5/2-f; GDPR m.6/1-f).
  • Destek taleplerin: bize e-posta ile yazdığında, talebini yanıtlamak için (meşru menfaat).

  • Abonelik kayıtları, yasal saklama yükümlülükleri ve abonelik süresince RevenueCat/Apple/Google'da tutulur.

WhispMe – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Data controller

Nuri Murat Dereli (developer and publisher of WhispMe, individual developer)
Istanbul, Türkiye
Email: whispme.destek@gmail.com

2. In short

Your videos, scripts and settings are stored only on your phone. WhispMe has no server of its own, requires no account, and none of this content is sent to us. The black-screen prompter mode is free and ad-free. Camera recording is unlocked by watching a rewarded ad (Google AdMob) or with a Premium subscription (Apple App Store / Google Play, via RevenueCat).

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Camera and microphone: for the preview and video recording. Recordings are saved to your gallery and the app's own folder.
  • Photos / gallery: to save your recordings, pick a video to trim, and delete recordings when you ask.
  • Location (optional): to note on your device where a recording started.
  • Motion sensors: to detect portrait or landscape; not stored.
  • Scripts, settings and free-time status: in the app's local storage.

5. Purposes and legal bases

  • Subscription and Premium access: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Personalised ads: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5/1), collected via App Tracking Transparency on iPhone and Google's consent form in the EU/UK. Without consent, non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Ad measurement, security and abuse prevention: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • Support requests: to answer you when you email us (legitimate interests).

6. International transfers

Google's and RevenueCat's servers are located outside Türkiye (mainly in the US and the EU). When these services are used, the data above is transferred abroad on the basis of the standard contractual clauses and safeguards offered by these providers and/or your consent. When you use the black-screen prompter without ads, no data is transferred.

7. Retention

  • On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app (videos saved to your gallery stay there).
  • Ad data is limited to the periods in Google's policies.
  • Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat/Apple/Google for the subscription period and legal retention obligations.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after your request is resolved.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to data portability. Send requests to whispme.destek@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. For subscription-related requests, including the ID shown under Settings > "My user ID" speeds things up. You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (in Türkiye: kvkk.gov.tr).

You can withdraw ad consent anytime: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking (iPhone) or Settings > Google > Ads (Android). Premium shows no ads at all.

9. Children

WhispMe is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
